## 1. REGA advertising compliance (what every Bona ad/listing must carry)

### The rule set (2026 change)
- **Regulatory Bylaw on Real Estate Marketing & Advertising (اللائحة التنظيمية للتسويق والإعلانات العقارية)** — 12 articles, dated 14/11/1447 (= 1 May 2026), issued under the Real Estate Brokerage Law (Royal Decree M/130, 30/11/1443). Art. 12: effective from gazette publication. Replaces the older "ضوابط الإعلانات العقارية". **[OFFICIAL]** Umm Al-Qura gazette: https://www.uqn.gov.sa/decisions-and-regulations/authorities/4000857 ; REGA copy: https://rega.gov.sa/الأنظمة-واللوائح-والأدلة/اللوائح/اللائحة-التنظيمية-للتسويق-والإعلانات-العقارية/ ; Argaam 2 May 2026: https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1901438 ; draft text (Nov 2025): https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Municipality/rega/RegulatoryBylawonRealEstateMarketingandAdve/Pages/default.aspx
- **Scope (Art. 3):** applies to *all* channels — social media (explicitly incl. Snapchat/Instagram/TikTok/X/YouTube), WhatsApp/Telegram groups, websites/portals, billboards, exhibitions. **[OFFICIAL gazette; BLOG Raghdan lists platforms]**

### Per-ad licence ("ترخيص إعلان عقاري")
- One licence **per advertisement**, issued electronically on REGA's **FAL platform (منصة فال)** — there is no separate "مُعلن" platform; owners can also self-license on licensed portals (Aqar etc.). **[OFFICIAL]** https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/eservices/real-estate-advertisement-license-issuance-service/ ; my.gov.sa/ar/services/20735
- **Beneficiaries:** individuals, brokers, owners, establishments → an *individual* FAL holder can issue ad licences without a CR. **[OFFICIAL REGA service page]**
- **Prerequisites:** active FAL licence + **registered brokerage contract with "marketing scope" (عقد وساطة يتضمن نطاق التسويق)** approved by the owner (broker enters owner mobile; owner approves via Nafath/Absher OTP) + ownership document (electronic deed no.). **[OFFICIAL REGA + Aqar help]** https://help.aqar.fm/ar/articles/8015333
- **Flow (12 steps):** login (Nafath) → Advertisement Licences → new → pick brokerage contract → pick deed → purpose (sale/rent) → **select advertising channels** → enter price → assign responsible employee → review → acknowledge → save. **[OFFICIAL]**
- **Fee:** ~**SAR 50 per licence**, packages (bulk) discounted up to ~70% for establishments (REGA page says "depends on service"; there is a "Purchase of advertising licence packages" e-service). **[BLOG Raghdan 2026 + OFFICIAL package service]** https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/eservices/purchase-of-advertising-license-packages/
- **Validity:** tied to the brokerage-contract period (blog); earlier REGA comms: 1 year extendable to 5. Ad must be removed on expiry. **[BLOG/OFFICIAL-news]**
- **Public verification:** anyone can check an ad licence by licence no., contract no., or deed no. **[OFFICIAL]** https://rega.gov.sa/en/media-center/news-announcements/rega-launches-real-estate-advertisement-license-inquiry-service/

### Mandatory content on every ad (Art. 5) — put these on every listing page, IG post, Snap, brochure
1. Rights being advertised (sale/rent) 2. Description & condition 3. Location 4. Services/encumbrances/disputes 5. **Contact data identical to the licence application** 6. **Advertiser name & capacity** 7. **Ad-licence number** 8. **Licence expiry date** + **practitioner (FAL) licence number**. A **QR code** may substitute the detailed data (e.g., on Snaps/billboards). **[OFFICIAL gazette]**
- Art. 7 prohibitions: defaming others, **fake listings to harvest leads**, contact details that don't match the licensed advertiser, government logos, photos/descriptions that contradict reality. **[OFFICIAL]**

### Penalties
- Advertising without licence / unlicensed brokerage: **up to SAR 200,000** (REGA spokesperson, Argaam 2025) ; platforms up to **SAR 1M**; graduated: correction → warning → fine → suspension ≤1 yr → revocation; fines can double on repeat within 3 years (blog). **[OFFICIAL-news + BLOG]** https://www.argaam.com/ar/article/articledetail/id/1700847
- Hotline 199011; REGA field + electronic monitoring of social media.

### Off-plan (NHC / Bin Saedan units) — different track
- Marketers of **Wafi-licensed off-plan projects are exempt from per-ad licences** but must cite the **project's Wafi licence number** (and escrow account no.) in every ad. **[OFFICIAL Argaam/REGA; Tamimi]** https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/wafi-program-off-plan-sales-from-a-developers-perspective-and-etnam-developer-registration/
- Marketing off-plan without the project licence: fines up to SAR 10M / 5 yrs (developer side). Brokers who market SME off-plan projects must be in **Wafi's broker registry** (CR required; qualifying programme; 1-yr then 3-yr; written developer pre-approval; reservation ≤5% into escrow). **[OFFICIAL istitlaa]** https://istitlaa.ncc.gov.sa/ar/Municipality/wafi/RealEstateBrokers/Pages/default.aspx
- Practical: get each developer's Wafi licence no. + a signed marketing contract before posting their units.

### Can a sole FAL-licensed individual operate under a brand ("Bona")?
- **Individual FAL** (رخصة فال للأفراد): Saudi, 18+, clean record, 3 SREI courses; **no CR needed**; ~SAR 300/yr. **Establishment FAL**: CR with activity **682010** (real-estate brokerage), professional responsible person holding an individual licence, office/national address, chamber membership; ~SAR 1,000/yr. **[OFFICIAL REGA FAL page (conditions) + BLOG for fees]** https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/platforms/fal-real-estate-brokerage/ ; https://mhabh.sa/ar/blog/رخصة-فال-العقارية/ ; https://roshem.sa/blog/fal-marketer-license-saudi
- Consequence: ads must show the **licensed advertiser's name** = the individual's name, and contact data must match the licence. Using "Bona" as the advertiser name with only an individual FAL conflicts with Art. 5/7 (mismatch) → to trade as Bona you need **trade name → CR (sole establishment) → establishment FAL** (and then the ad's advertiser = Bona + its FAL no.). Until then: brand the site "Bona" but state "الوسيط المرخص: <name> — رخصة فال 1100313556" on every ad. **[Inference from OFFICIAL Art. 5/7]**
- **Trade Names Law (in force 3 Apr 2025)** now allows **English/Latin-letter names** — "Bona" is reservable (60-day reservation via Saudi Business Center). **[OFFICIAL MoC]** https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/17-09-24-03.aspx ; https://business.sa/en/eservices/details/e2162d09-08ef-4011-1064-08dd6aa798b2
- **Ejar** (rentals): broker registration requires **CR with 682010 + national address + course + FAL in the establishment's name** → an individual FAL cannot document leases on Ejar. **[OFFICIAL Ejar support replies + REGA registration page]** https://x.com/ejar_sa/status/1873015145451921777 ; https://rega.gov.sa/rega-services/eservices/تسجيل-منشأة-عقارية-وسيط-عقاري-فرد-لأول-مرة/
- **Nafath** is the identity layer for FAL login, owner approval of brokerage contracts, Ejar, and (Art. 8) platforms must verify advertisers via national SSO. Bona's own site listing only its own contracted properties is *not* a "platform"; if it ever hosts third-party listings, Art. 8 (KSA hosting, Nafath, REGA integration, SDAIA registration) applies. **[OFFICIAL]**
- Commission cap: **2.5%** of sale price (or first-year rent) unless agreed otherwise in writing. **[OFFICIAL law via Okaz]** https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2110786

## 2. PDPL — analytics, pixels, WhatsApp, AI concierge

- PDPL in force 14 Sep 2023; enforcement grace ended **14 Sep 2024**; regulator SDAIA. **[OFFICIAL via DLA Piper, updated 11 Feb 2026]** https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=SA
- **Legal bases:** consent is primary; contract, legal obligation, and a limited **legitimate interest** (2023 amendment; not for sensitive data). **[BLOG law firm CMS]** https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-data-protection-and-cyber-security-laws/saudi-arabia
- **Cookies/pixels:** no cookie-specific law; cookies/pixels are personal-data processing under PDPL → **opt-in consent for non-essential cookies (GA4, Meta/Snap/TikTok pixels)** is the safe reading; strictly-necessary exempt. **[CMS; vendor pages CookieHub/CookieYes]** → **Yes, a consent banner (AR/EN) is required in practice**; wire it to Google Consent Mode v2 and to pixel firing.
- **Privacy notice (Art. 12 + Implementing Regs):** must be shown **before** collection and state: controller identity/contact, purposes, legal basis, data categories, **collection methods (incl. cookies)**, recipients/processors, **cross-border transfers**, retention, rights & how to exercise, complaint route. SDAIA issued the **"Elaboration and Developing Privacy Policy Guideline" with a standard template (30 Aug 2024)**. **[OFFICIAL SDAIA guideline; PDF blocked by WAF — download via dgp.sdaia.gov.sa Knowledge Center]** https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/22389-published-sdaia-elaboration-and-developing-privacy-policy-guideline
- **Direct marketing (Art. 25/26):** prior consent + easy opt-out for marketing messages (WhatsApp broadcasts, SMS, email). Draft 2025 amendments keep consent and drop the "sender identity in every message" rule. **[OFFICIAL law; Clyde & Co on May 2025 consultation]** https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2025/05/saudi-arabia-new-pdp-law-consultation
- **Controller registration (NDGP, dgp.sdaia.gov.sa):** mandatory if processing sensitive data or core activity is personal-data processing; the **2025 draft adds "cross-border transfers" as a trigger** → with GA4/Meta/Retell, assume registration is required (free, online). **[OFFICIAL Morgan Lewis/Clyde]** https://www.morganlewis.com/blogs/sourcingatmorganlewis/2024/12/saudi-arabias-personal-data-protection-law-a-guide-to-registering-as-a-data-controller
- **Cross-border (GA4 / Meta / Retell = US):** Transfer Regulations (Sept 2024) allow transfer for the controller's operations / direct service to the data subject if: (a) adequacy — **no adequacy list published yet**, (b) **Saudi SCCs** (modular templates), BCRs, or certification, plus a **documented risk assessment** (Feb 2025 guideline) and disclosure in the notice. Sensitive data can't use the simplified path. Penalties: up to SAR 5M admin; unlawful transfer criminal up to 1 yr / SAR 1M. **[OFFICIAL via King & Spalding, ITIF Jun 2025, Clyde Mar 2025]** https://www.kslaw.com/news-and-insights/international-personal-data-transfers-under-saudi-arabias-data-protection-law ; https://itif.org/publications/2025/06/09/saudi-arabia-cross-border-data-transfer-regulation/
- **Practical minimum for Bona:** (1) AR/EN privacy notice from the SDAIA template naming Google, Meta, Snap, Retell, WhatsApp as recipients + "transfers outside KSA under SCC-equivalent safeguards"; (2) cookie banner with categories; (3) a one-page transfer risk assessment on file; (4) NDGP registration; (5) WhatsApp marketing only to opted-in numbers; (6) **AI concierge: announce "this call is recorded / AI assistant" at the start**, log consent per call, minimise retention, store transcripts on KSA/own infra. Call-recording guidance: pre-call announcement + consent + local storage. **[BLOG Blue Edge, Jul 2026]** https://b-edgetech.com/articles/call-recording-compliance
- Breach notification: 72h to SDAIA. **[OFFICIAL via DLA Piper]**

## 4. Meta specifics

- **Asset chain:** IG → Professional (Business) account → link to a Facebook Page → add both to a **Meta Business Portfolio** → **Business Verification** (upload **CR**; legal name must match — #1 rejection cause in GCC) → create **System User** → generate token with `ads_management, pages_*, instagram_*, whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_manage_events`. IG can belong to only one portfolio. **[Meta help via Leadsie/Properti guides; wa-pixel GCC blog Jul 2026]** https://wa-pixel.com/blog/whatsapp-green-tick-verification-gcc
- **Meta Verified / Meta One** business tiers are **piloting in KSA since late May 2026**: Essential $14.99/mo (~SAR 56, verified badge, impersonation protection), Advanced $49.99/mo (~SAR 187). **[BLOG Watily May 2026 / almcorp]** https://watily.com/blog/en/meta-whatsapp-paid-subscriptions-saudi-arabia-2026/
- **WhatsApp verified badge (now blue, formerly green):** requires Business Verification + Official Business Account request from a **WhatsApp Business Platform** number (BSP/Cloud API), or Meta Verified subscription. 2–8 weeks. **[BLOG getkanal/wa-pixel]**
- **Click-to-WhatsApp attribution — official mechanics:** Meta injects `messages[0].referral { ctwa_clid, source_id, source_type, headline, body, ... }` in the **Cloud API webhook** (On-Premises API EOL 23 Oct 2025). To close the loop you send **Conversions API for Business Messaging** events with `action_source="business_messaging"`, `messaging_channel="whatsapp"`, `user_data.ctwa_clid` (unhashed), event names `LeadSubmitted`, `QualifiedLead`, `Purchase`, `ViewContent`, etc. **A dataset must be created against the WABA ID** with a token holding `whatsapp_business_management` + `whatsapp_business_manage_events`. **[OFFICIAL Meta docs]** https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/business-messaging
- **Unofficial API (Baileys/Evolution):** the WhatsApp Web protocol *does* expose the click ID — `ContextInfo.externalAdReply.ctwaClid` (field 13), `conversionSource` (18, e.g. "FB_Ads"), `entryPointConversionSource` (29, "ctwa_ad"), `ctwaSignals` (54), `ctwaPayload` (55). **[OFFICIAL Baileys WAProto.proto]** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WhiskeySockets/Baileys/master/WAProto/WAProto.proto — so a Baileys session can *see* which ad a lead came from. Caveats: (a) Evolution API v2.3.7 dropped the referral in its `messages.upsert` (issue #2645) — check version/raw payload; (b) **CAPI for Business Messaging requires a WABA-bound dataset; sending ctwa_clid harvested from a non-Platform number is undocumented and likely unattributed** (unverified); (c) unofficial clients violate WhatsApp ToS → ban risk on the only lead channel. https://github.com/evolution-foundation/evolution-api/issues/2645
- **WhatsApp Cloud API cost (KSA):** per-message since 1 Jul 2025; **service replies inside the 24h window are free; CTWA opens a 72h free-entry-point window** where all messages are free; marketing ≈ SAR 0.35 / utility ≈ SAR 0.15 per message (blog); SAR billing available. **[OFFICIAL Meta pricing page + BLOG quali-d/blueticks]** https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing
- **Lead Ads (instant forms):** fine for off-plan "register interest"; must link the PDPL privacy notice; pipe leads via webhook (n8n) into CRM within minutes; quality lower than CTWA for luxury.
- **Recommendation:** run all paid CTWA traffic to a **Cloud API number** (new or migrated), keep Baileys only for ops if needed; fire `LeadSubmitted` on first reply and `QualifiedLead` on viewing booked.

## 5. Google

- **Advertiser verification (KSA):** organisation = CR extract / business licence / VAT cert + representative's ID; individual = Saudi ID/passport; must match payments profile. **[OFFICIAL]** https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9872280?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DSA
- **Housing personalised-ads restrictions (no age/gender/ZIP targeting) apply to US & Canada only** — KSA targeting is unrestricted by Google; REGA rules still require licence no. on ad/landing page. **[OFFICIAL]** https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/9997418?hl=en
- **Google Business Profile:** category "Real estate agent" (solo) or "Real estate agency"; **video verification**; can hide address as a service-area business (Jeddah districts); bilingual description; add FAL number in description. **[Google community/guide blogs]** https://support.google.com/business/thread/433401598/real-estate-agent-business-profile-verification?hl=en
- **GA4 + Google tag:** GA4 (IP never stored) via GTM, Consent Mode v2 driven by the banner, UTMs on every wa.me link, events `whatsapp_click`, `call_click`, `listing_view`, `tour_view`, `brochure_download`, `lead_form_submit`.
- **Server-side GTM:** Google Cloud **me-central2 (Dammam)** exists but KSA-billed customers must buy through CNTXT — heavy for a solo; alternatives: self-host sGTM Docker on own VPS or Stape. Start client-side; add sGTM once spend > ~SAR 10k/mo. **[OFFICIAL Google Cloud docs]** https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/dammam-region-access
- **Search Console:** DNS-verify the root domain; submit EN/AR sitemaps with hreflang; keep IndexNow for Bing.

## 6. Snapchat & TikTok — worth it for a solo luxury boutique?

Test budget **SAR 3–5k/mo** (agencies say SAR 5–10k/mo with 2–3 ad sets; floor ~SAR 1.5–3k/mo per line). Post organically (repurpose Reels) now; add Events API + Smart+ later for off-plan launches. **[BLOG Stackmatix/Hovi 2026]** https://thehovi.com/blog/industry-guides/tiktok-marketing-saudi-arabia-business-guide-2026
