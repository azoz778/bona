## Executive summary — the five things that will actually move followers, ranked

**1. Volume on TikTok, in Arabic — 10 posts a week, done by hand.**
TikTok is the only platform whose own documentation says follower count is *not* a ranking
factor and that region *is* a hard distribution rule. It is where a zero-follower account
gets shown to strangers. Buffer's 2.1M-post dataset says reach **per post** rises 24% at
10+ posts a week versus 1–2, and follower growth runs 5.5× — posting more does not
cannibalise. Be realistic about what this costs: **TikTok and Snapchat cannot be
automated at all** (TikTok's unaudited API publishes private-only; Snapchat has no organic
posting API), so this is roughly **30 minutes a day of a human pasting from a queue**,
plus a 2–3 hour production block each Saturday. Nothing else here works if that habit does
not hold.

**2. Never post the same file to two platforms. Re-export for each.**
This is the best-evidenced item in the whole document and the easiest to get wrong.
Instagram says plainly that third-party watermarks and already-posted content suppress
reach, and on **30 April 2026 it extended that penalty from Reels to photos and
carousels** — borders and credits explicitly do not fix it. Shoot once, export clean
masters, then change the first three seconds, the audio and the caption per platform. Two
extra minutes in the editor is the difference between being recommended and being buried.

**3. Make faceless the format, not a workaround.**
Property-only footage, an Arabic text hook burned into the first 1.5 seconds, ambient
sound, one consistent grade. The brand promise is already *«بهدوء»* — a presenter would
contradict the product, and no-face is what makes daily posting physically possible. The
trust gap that a faceless account has in a licensed profession is closed deliberately: the
FAL number in every bio and on every post, and an education pillar that is a third of the
calendar.

**4. Take Snapchat seriously as the second organic surface — this is the KSA-specific edge.**
Snapchat reaches **89% of Saudi adults** against Instagram's 71%, and is the least
male-skewed major platform in the Kingdom (43.5% female) — which matters when the property
decision is joint. More importantly, Snapchat's own ranking documentation describes exactly
the cold-start mechanism a new account needs: Spotlight shows content from creators a
viewer has never seen *"based on statistics — looking at the creator's country, language,
age and gender."* A free business Public Profile is discoverable in Search and Discover
from day one. Most brokers treat Snapchat as a paid channel here. It is also an organic one.

**5. Post about Jeddah, not about your listings — and when something works, repeat it.**
District guides and buyer/seller education outperform listing posts, are what people
actually save and send, and cannot require a REGA advertising licence. Then the discipline
most accounts fail: when a video does 10× your median, the instinct is to make something
new; the correct move is to re-shoot that exact format with five different properties.

### The ranking

**1. TikTok — the cold-start engine, and it is not close.**
- **[PLATFORM]** *"neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system"* — https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you
- **[PLATFORM]** Region is a hard distribution rule, not merely a signal: *"we have rules in place that ensure videos in your For You feed include content from creators in your region"* — https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/recommendation-system. The same page names *"The hashtags associated with the video"* and the language of the author as ranking inputs — which makes **Arabic captions and hashtags a genuine Saudi-targeting lever here**.
- **[VENDOR]** Metricool 2026 study, 2,314,756 posts across 92,000+ accounts: **44% of accounts under 100K followers grew** year on year, and **the For You page drives 7 of every 10 views** — https://metricool.com/tiktok-study/

**2. Snapchat — the platform this brief under-rated, and the KSA-specific opportunity.**
This is the biggest correction to my earlier reasoning. Snapchat's own ranking
documentation describes exactly the cold-start mechanism a new account needs:
- **[PLATFORM]** *"past engagement with other content will (of course) be unimportant for a new user"*, and Spotlight uses *"Your country, the language that you speak, your age and gender so that we can show you local content"*, and explicitly: *"We may show you content from a creator whose content you haven't watched before because we know based on statistics (looking at the creator's country, language, age and gender) that the creator is typically liked by other users who share a similar demographic as you"* — https://help.snapchat.com/hc/en-us/articles/8961653169940-How-We-Rank-Content-on-Spotlight
- **[PLATFORM]** Business Public Profiles are **free and permanent**, and *"instantly discoverable throughout Snapchat — Snapchatters can find your business through Search, @ mentions, Discover, Lens Explorer"* — https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/blog/introducing-public-profiles
- **[PLATFORM]** Spotlight format rules are strict and suit this plan exactly: *"Snaps should be vertical videos with sound. Still-image photos, horizontal Snaps, blurry Snaps, and text-only Snaps won't show up in Spotlight"* — https://creators.snap.com/learn-shine-in-the-spotlight-spotlight-101
- **[NEWS]** Snap's KSA regional lead, Arab News, 12 Jul 2024: Snapchat reaches *"over 90 percent of those aged between 13 and 34"* in the Kingdom, opened *"over 50 times a day on average"* — https://www.arabnews.com/node/2548521/media

⚠️ One honest gap: **no best-time-to-post study exists for Snapchat from anyone**, Snap
included. Sprout, Buffer, Hootsuite and Metricool all exclude it.

**3. Instagram — the credibility surface, not the growth engine.**
Instagram publishes a genuine cold-start audition model — **[PLATFORM]** *"your reel is
initially shown to a small audience… The best-performing reels are then shown to a
slightly wider audience… designed to level the playing field by distributing content
based on its engagement, not just the creator's follower count"*
(https://creators.instagram.com/how-to-grow) — but its own canonical ranking page still
lists *"popularity signals such as number of followers"* among Reels inputs
(https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained), and the
measured outcome is stark: **[VENDOR]** Metricool's 2026 Instagram study (24,364,803
posts, 375,118 accounts) found **only 21% of accounts under 10,000 followers grew**,
against 44% of sub-100K TikTok accounts, with Reels reach down **35% year on year** —
https://metricool.com/press-release-instagram-study-2026/

🔴 **Trial Reels is not available to you at launch.** **[PLATFORM]** Eligibility requires
**at least 200 followers** on a professional account —
https://help.instagram.com/835643311711702/ It is a phase-2 tool.

Instagram is still where Jeddah's affluent 30–55 segment lives and where DM-to-WhatsApp is
shortest. Treat it as the place the audience converts, not the place it is acquired.

**4. YouTube Shorts — cheap option value, under-documented.**
79.2% population reach and a genuine search long tail; a district guide posted today can
still be answering «الشاطئ ولا أبحر» in a year. **[VENDOR]** Metricool measured YouTube
views per video **+30% YoY**, the only format-level growth in its study. But YouTube
publishes **no cold-start documentation and no Shorts ranking document** — the widely
repeated "50–500 viewer test pool" is folklore with no platform source. Post it because
the marginal cost is minutes, and write real Arabic titles and descriptions, because on
YouTube the metadata *is* the distribution.

**5. X — worst reach per unit of effort here, despite real KSA numbers.**
**[PLATFORM]** X's open-sourced algorithm reserves roughly half the timeline for accounts
the viewer already follows: *"~50% of posts come from this candidate source"* (in-network)
— https://github.com/twitter/the-algorithm. That is structurally the opposite of what a
zero-follower account needs. KSA is one of X's strongest markets but it is **shrinking
(−6.8% YoY)**, and **[VENDOR]** Socialinsider puts X engagement at **0.10–0.12%**, the
lowest measured anywhere. It also cannot share your schedule — Buffer's 8.7M-tweet dataset
puts X's peak at **mid-morning 9–11am on weekdays**, the inverse of every other platform.

**Facebook: create the Page, never post to it.** You need it to unlock Instagram Graph API
publishing and Meta ads. It is **75.7% male** — the most male-skewed platform in the
Kingdom — and **[VENDOR]** Socialinsider measures Page engagement at **0.15%**.

**LinkedIn: skip for B2C property.** 75.3% male, the 12.0M figure is *registered members*
not monthly actives (DataReportal's own caveat: *"not directly comparable"*), and its
distribution model is person-branded — doubly wrong for a faceless account. Legitimate
only for B2B: developers, family offices, relocation and HR.

### Revised operational conclusion

Put the volume on **TikTok**, take **Snapchat** seriously as the second organic surface
rather than a paid afterthought, use **Instagram** to convert, and **YouTube** to accrue.

⚠️ **But do not "post the same file everywhere" — see §4.** Instagram's originality
penalty is documented, live, and was *extended* on 30 April 2026. Each platform needs its
own export.

## 3. The 30-day plan (Wed 9 Sep – Thu 8 Oct 2026)

### How the 30 days are built

**Ten TikToks a week, re-exported for every other platform.** The tables below list an
**Asset A** and an **Asset B** for each day. Asset A is non-negotiable and goes out every
day; **Asset B is the stretch slot — drop it on Friday and Saturday** and you land on 10
posts a week, the top band in Buffer's frequency data. Instagram Reels, Snapchat and
YouTube Shorts draw from the same shoots.

⚠️ **They are not the same *file*.** Instagram suppresses watermarked and
already-posted content, and extended that penalty on 30 April 2026 (§4). Each platform
gets its own export: different first three seconds, different audio, different caption.

**And almost none of it can be automated.** TikTok's unaudited API publishes private-only;
Snapchat has no organic posting API; X write access costs $200/month; the LinkedIn tool on
this machine can only send DMs. **Only YouTube — and Instagram Reels once the Page link in
`marketing/instagram-status.md` is done — can be scheduled.** Everything else is a person
pasting from a queue: about **30 minutes a day**, plus the Saturday block.

**If 30 minutes a day does not hold, drop to 5 posts a week and keep it.** A cadence
abandoned in week 3 is worse than a smaller one sustained — restarting a cold account
wastes the warm-up.

Production is batched on the Saudi weekend: one 2–3 hour session every **Saturday**
produces the coming week's assets from the **318 stills already in `public/listings/`** via
the `tk-walkthrough` pipeline (photos → cinematic 9:16, ≈$2–8 of fal.ai credit per
property), plus whatever arrived through the WhatsApp video-intake daemon.

Day 1 is **Wednesday 9 September 2026**; day 30 is **Thursday 8 October 2026**. The Saudi
weekend is Friday–Saturday; the work week is Sunday–Thursday. **Primary posting slot
20:30–23:00 AST, second post 00:00–01:00** (§5).

**The five pillars** — the rotation that keeps the account legal and the feed varied:

| Code | Pillar | Share | Ad licence needed? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RULES** | Buyer/seller education — deeds, escrow, RETT, the 2.5% commission cap, Wafi, FAL | 30% | No |
| **DISTRICT** | Jeddah district guides — Al Shati, Al Rawdah, Obhur, Al Basateen, Al Nuzhah, Al Khalidiyah | 25% | No |
| **EYE** | Sensory detail — light, material, one room, ambient sound | 20% | **Only if the property is unlicensed** — see below |
| **WALK** | Full walkthrough of a named, licensed listing | 15% | **Yes** |
| **OWNER** | Seller-side — discretion, why listings go stale, photography | 10% | No |

Note the shape: **RULES + DISTRICT + OWNER = 65% of the calendar can never require an
advertising licence**, because there is no property to license.

⚠️ **The trap that shapes everything else.** EYE and WALK both show property Bona
markets, and under the bylaw that makes them advertisements **regardless of whether a
price, a location or a call to action appears** (§6). Cropping to an anonymous corner of
a room makes it *worse*, not better. The fix is not to avoid property footage — it is to
**issue advertising licences for the 5–7 featured Jeddah listings in week 1**. Art. 5(2)
then lets a Reel carry nothing but a licence number and a QR code, and the entire
318-image library becomes usable. Until those licences exist, week 1 runs on genuinely
property-free content, which is exactly what the calendar below does.

**Why faceless is an advantage here, not a compromise**

1. The brand promise is already *«بهدوء»* — quietly. A presenter on camera contradicts the product; discretion *is* what an off-market seller is buying.
2. Luxury property media convention is that **the home is the subject**. Architectural Digest tours, developer films and the top international brokerages all shoot property-first.
3. No face removes the single biggest production blocker — owner availability, appearance, confidence on camera in Arabic — which is what actually lets you sustain two posts a day. Cadence beats charisma on a cold account.
4. A faceless asset is **evergreen and delegable**: it can be re-cut, re-captioned, re-posted a year later, and produced by anyone.
5. It travels across five platforms unchanged. A talking-head does not — it has to be reshot for each aspect ratio and audience.

The one real weakness — a faceless account in a **licensed profession** has a trust
deficit — is closed deliberately: the FAL number is in every bio, the disclosure block
is on every property post, and the RULES pillar (30% of everything) exists precisely to
demonstrate expertise without a face.

## 4. Growth mechanics that are still open in 2026


### 1. Never cross-post the same file — this is the best-evidenced item in this document

**[PLATFORM]** Instagram, live and current: *"Watermarks: Ditch the watermark! The 3rd
party watermark on your reel may be impacting its reach"* —
https://creators.instagram.com/faq · and *"Having third-party watermarks on your reels
may prevent them from being recommended to new audiences"* —
https://creators.instagram.com/how-to-grow

**[PLATFORM]** The original 2023 statement still stands unretracted: *"We aim to make
certain reels less visible… low-resolution or watermarked reels, reels that are muted or
contain borders, reels that are majority text, or reels that have already been posted on
Instagram."*

**[PLATFORM]** And it escalated on **30 April 2026**: *"Accounts that primarily reup other
people's work in image posts and carousel posts will no longer be eligible for
recommendations"* — the originality penalty extended from Reels to photos and carousels,
with borders, watermarks and caption credits **explicitly insufficient**. Recovery comes
*"when most recently posted images, carousels and Reels are considered original within a
30-day period"* — https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram

**[PLATFORM]** TikTok bans duplicates too: content is *"ineligible for the FYF if it
includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new"* —
https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity

**➡️ The operating rule: shoot once, export clean masters, re-edit per platform.** Change
the first three seconds, the audio bed and the caption. Two extra minutes per platform in
the editor buys the difference between being recommended and being suppressed. **This is
the single most consequential production rule in this document** and it invalidates the
"one asset, five destinations, paste everywhere" shortcut.

### 2. Instagram hashtags are now hard-capped at 5 — your existing file is out of date

**[PLATFORM]** Instagram's official @Creators account, **18 December 2025**: *"New hashtag
guidance: Starting today, Instagram will allow up to 5 hashtags in a reel or post"* —
https://www.threads.com/@creators/post/DSalXGPCWM4 · **[NEWS]**
https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/

Hashtag *following* was removed in **November 2024** — **[NEWS]**
https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagrams-removing-option-follow-hashtags/733155/

🔴 **`marketing/hashtags.md` currently instructs "15–20 per post". That is no longer
possible on Instagram and must be rewritten** — 5 targeted tags for Instagram, and keep
the long Arabic sets **for TikTok and Snapchat**, where hashtags remain *documented
ranking inputs* (TikTok Transparency Center lists them; Snapchat lists them as Spotlight
Story metadata). The asymmetry is real and worth exploiting: the Arabic district tags that
do nothing on Instagram are a genuine targeting lever on TikTok.

⚠️ **[NONE]** There is **no evidence base at all** on Arabic-vs-English hashtag
performance — no controlled test, no platform statement, no disclosed-methodology study.
If it matters, run your own: same creative, Arabic-tag vs English-tag vs no-tag, at least
20 pairs.

### 3. Instagram Collab posts — the strongest audience-borrow primitive available

**[PLATFORM]** Up to **5 collaborators** (6 accounts total). *"If the other account
accepts, the post will also show on their profile and be distributed to their followers in
Instagram feed"*, and engagement is pooled rather than duplicated —
https://help.instagram.com/291200585956732 · Instagram recommends it explicitly as a reach
tactic: *"These posts can be recommended in places like the Reels tab, Explore, and Feed"*
— https://creators.instagram.com/blog/tips-for-improving-your-reach

⚠️ Asymmetry to accept: the original author owns the post. If they delete it or deactivate,
it disappears from your grid too. At zero followers you will always be the invitee — which
is fine, it is exactly the point.

**By contrast, TikTok Stitch and Duet do NOT lend you the other account's audience.**
**[PLATFORM]** *"The Duet will only appear on the profile of the person who recorded the
Duet"* — https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7636670084584512021 You get topical
association and a backlink, not feed placement. **Instagram Collab is structurally the
stronger borrow; put the outreach effort there.**

### 4. TikTok Search is a real, instrumented discovery surface

**[PLATFORM]** *"Search powers discovery on TikTok"* — and Creator Search Insights
surfaces **content-gap topics**, those *"searched for often, but aren't featured in a large
number of videos"* — https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/creator-search-insights

For a Jeddah brokerage this is the most directly actionable tool in the whole report:
Arabic district queries are almost certainly a content gap. ⚠️ The *"Searches by
followers"* filter needs **1,000 followers**; below that you still get content-gap and
general topic data.

### 5. Comment-driven distribution — one half is documented, the other is not

**[PLATFORM]** Replying to comments on **your own** content is officially endorsed with a
stated window: *"Like or authentically reply to comments on your reel within the first 7
days to generate more engagement, which can help your content get more reach"* —
https://creators.instagram.com/faq · and **profile visits are a confirmed ranking input**:
*"how much engagement per viewer the content has received (through actions such as watch
time, sharing, following, liking, commenting, profile visits, etc.)"*

🔴 **[NONE]** But *"commenting on larger local accounts drives profile visits"* — the
tactic in the day-by-day plan — **has no evidence behind it whatsoever.** The mechanism is
plausible and the ingredient (profile visits) is confirmed, but nobody has measured it.
It is cheap, so keep it; do not budget against it as a certainty.

### 6. "Comment X and I'll DM you" — compliant and effective. DM blasting is not.

**[PLATFORM]** Meta's Messaging Platform policy permits business messages only inside a
**24-hour window opened by a user action**, and Sponsored Messages / One-Time Notification
are **not available on the Instagram Messaging API**. There is no compliant API route to
DM people who have not messaged you first.

**➡️ «اكتب "فيلا" وأرسل لك الكتيب» is compliant and works.** Cold DM blasting risks the
account. This is a good fit for the brochure library that already exists.

### 7. WhatsApp Channels — genuinely faceless-friendly, and it can acquire, not just retain

**[PLATFORM]** Channels have a real recommender, not just a subscriber list. Ranking
inputs include *"Number of followers; Reactions to channel updates; Frequency of channel
updates; Similarity of content"* plus *"Your general location information (e.g.
country/city) and language preference"* — https://faq.whatsapp.com/962978635456336
Country and language feeding a discovery surface means it **acquires**. It is
rich-get-richer, so seed it from the other channels.

**[PLATFORM]** And it is unusually well-suited to a discreet brand: *"Followers won't see
your WhatsApp profile name, picture, or your phone number"*, and *"People viewing a
channel can't see who is following a channel"* — https://faq.whatsapp.com/549900560675125
For a brokerage whose promise is *«بهدوء»*, a channel where buyers can follow without
revealing themselves to each other is an unusually good product fit.

**🇸🇦 [REGULATOR]** CST's *Saudi Internet 2025* report: **WhatsApp is the most-used platform
in the Kingdom — 94.4% of male and 90.4% of female internet users** —
https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/knowledge-center/reports ⚠️ **[NONE]** for a KSA-specific
*Channels* adoption figure.

### 8. Google Business Profile — a parallel funnel, not a social amplifier

**[PLATFORM]** Google's published local ranking factors are relevance, distance and
prominence; **posts are not among them** — https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091
🔴 **[NONE]** that GBP activity influences Instagram or TikTok distribution in any way.
Worth doing for local search on its own merits. Note it is structurally incompatible with

### 9. Trending audio — Instagram says it matters, and then blocks the tool

**[PLATFORM]** *"We consider the popularity of trends on Reels, including music and

## 5. Cadence and timing


### The constraint that decides the cadence: almost none of this can be automated

Checked on this machine, 2026-09-08:

| Platform | Automatable? | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | ✅ Yes | The only fully automatable destination |
| **Instagram** Reels/feed | 🟡 Yes, once the Page link is done | Graph API publishing works after the owner's 10-minute click-through in `marketing/instagram-status.md`. **Stories cannot be reliably automated** |
| **TikTok** | ❌ No | The unaudited Content Posting API publishes **private-only**. Auditing is not worth it at this scale |
| **Snapchat** | ❌ No | **No organic posting API exists at all** |
| **X** | ❌ Not affordably | Write access is $200/month for this volume |
| **LinkedIn** | ❌ No | The MCP on this machine can send DMs only — it has no post tool |

**So the plan has to be executable by a human pasting from a queue**, every day, on a
phone. That is the real budget, and it caps the cadence far more than any algorithm does.
Roughly: **3 minutes per TikTok post, 5 minutes for the Snapchat Story, 5 minutes for
Instagram Stories, 15 minutes of comment replies — about 30 minutes a day**, plus the
Saturday production block. Anything that does not fit in 30 minutes a day will not happen
in week 3, whatever this document says.

### Frequency — more posting does not cannibalise reach

The best-sampled evidence says the opposite of the common fear. **[VENDOR]** Buffer,
**2.1M posts across 102,000 Instagram accounts** —
https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/

| Posts/week | Reach **per post** vs baseline | Follower growth rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | baseline | +0.12% |
| 3–5 | +12% | +0.26% |
| 6–9 | +18% | +0.44% |
| **10+** | **+24%** | **+0.66% (5.5×)** |

Per-post reach **rises** with frequency — diminishing, never negative.

🔑 **The measurement trap that makes people believe otherwise:** per-post *engagement
rate* compresses as you post more, while reach per post and total engagement both rise. A
dashboard reporting engagement-rate-per-post after a cadence increase will show a fake
decline and talk you into posting less. **Track total reach and reach-per-post. Never
engagement-rate-per-post when changing cadence.**

⚠️ Shared bias worth stating: all frequency data comes from scheduling tools whose
commercial interest is you posting more. Consistent and well-sampled, but not disinterested.
**[VENDOR]** For context, Socialinsider's benchmark has brands averaging **5 posts/week**
on both Instagram and TikTok — so 10/week is genuinely differentiating.

### The cadence

| Platform | Target | Fallback if 30 min/day is not sustainable | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | **10/week** (1–2/day) | 5/week | Manual, from the queue |
| **Instagram Reels** | 5–7/week | 5/week | Automatable once the Page is linked |
| **Instagram Stories** | 3–5/day | 1/day | Manual |
| **Snapchat** | 1 Story/day + 2–3 Spotlight/week | 3 Stories/week | Manual |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 5–7/week | 5/week | Automatable |
| **X** | 2–3/week | 1/week | Manual, 10 min a week total |

**Start at the fallback column in week 1 and only scale up once the Saturday production
block is reliably producing.** A cadence you abandon in week 3 is worse than a smaller one
you keep — the platforms reward consistency, and restarting after a gap on a cold account
wastes the warm-up.

Remember §4: each platform needs its **own export**, not the same file. Budget the extra
two minutes per platform in the editor, not at the point of posting.

### Timing — and an honest note on how much it matters

**The best Saudi-specific source is the regulator, not a marketing study.** **[REGULATOR]**
CST *Saudi Internet Report 2024*, published May 2025 —
https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/N2025051200

> *"the peak hours of Internet usage span is from **9:00 PM to 11:00 PM**, and pinpoint **Saturday** as one of the busiest days of the week."*

Same report: 99% internet penetration, **mobile is 99.4% of all usage**, 48 GB/month per
person — three times the global average.

Three independent streams converge on the same window: CST's measured 9–11pm national
peak, the post-Isha cultural window, and the global 6–11pm short-form optimum.

**Prayer times anchor it.** Computed for Jeddah (21.49°N, 39.19°E), Umm al-Qura
convention: Maghrib **18:33** and **Isha 20:03** today, 8 Sep 2026; Maghrib 18:12 by
1 October. Isha moves about 90 minutes across the year, so **recompute quarterly**.

| Slot | Time (AST) | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | **20:30 – 23:00** | The main slot, every day. Post-Isha, inside CST's measured peak |
| **Secondary** | **00:00 – 01:00** | Second daily post. **[VENDOR]** Metricool found Reels get their highest views per Reel at midnight because competition collapses — 6pm sees 119,705 Reels posted against midnight's far thinner field. In a market with a documented late skew this arbitrage should be stronger. Cheap to test |
| Avoid | 18:00 – 20:15 | Straddles Maghrib and Isha |
| **Best days** | **Friday and Saturday** | The Saudi weekend; CST names Saturday among the busiest days. Thursday evening is the Saudi "Friday night" |
| National Day, Wed 23 Sep | **19:30 – 20:30** | Deliberately early — the feed saturates late that night |

⚠️ Note the day-of-week trap: Sprout Social's advice to *"avoid Saturday and Sunday"* was
computed against a **Monday–Friday** working week and is **actively wrong for Saudi
Arabia**, where the weekend is Friday–Saturday. Buffer's 7.1M-post TikTok dataset — which
finds **Saturday the single best day** — agrees with the Saudi signal.

**And the honest caveat, from Buffer's own conclusion:** *"Timing isn't as critical as it
was in the chronological-feed days… Think of it as the cherry on top of your strategy, not
the cake itself."* For a zero-follower account whose distribution is **entirely
recommendation-driven**, timing barely matters. Get roughly inside 20:30–23:00 and stop
optimising it. The format and the hook are the cake.

### Ramadan 2027 (~8 Feb – 8 Mar) — invert the schedule

**[NEWS]** Arab News, 15 Mar 2026: *"interaction peaks after Taraweeh prayers and again
before sahoor"*, while *"afternoon activity slows noticeably"* —
https://www.arabnews.com/node/2636507/saudi-arabia A Jeddah-based strategist in the same
piece: *"Highly polished content does not always perform well; simple, relatable videos
often connect more."*

**➡️ Publish 22:00–01:00 (post-Taraweeh) and 03:30–05:15 (pre-suhoor). Kill the afternoon.
Shift production values down, not up.**

### The weekly rhythm

- **Saturday** — production block, 2–3 hours, next week's assets, exported per platform.
- **Sunday–Thursday** — publish from the queue, reply to every comment within the hour.
- **Tuesday** — weekly review: watch-through on TikTok, sends-per-reach on Instagram, **total reach and reach-per-post**. Kill the weakest format, double the strongest.
- **Friday** — lightest production day, strongest evening slot.

## 6. Compliance — what Bona can legally post today


Prior research (`2026-09-06-saudi-re-acquisition-research.md` §1) covered the bylaw's
machinery. This section establishes the **content boundary**, from the actual gazetted
text, and **corrects the prior document on four points**.

**Not legal advice.** Have a Saudi-licensed lawyer sign this off before relying on it.

### The definition — and why "no price, no CTA" is not a loophole

**[OFFICIAL TEXT]** Umm Al-Qura gazette, published 2026-05-01 —
https://www.uqn.gov.sa/decisions-and-regulations/authorities/4000857

> **الإعلان العقاري:** أي محتوى منشور لترويج منتج عقاري في أي وسيلة مرئية أو مقروءة أو مسموعة.
> *Real-estate advertisement: any published content **for the promotion of a real-estate product** in any visual, readable or audible medium.*

The definition requires only **published content** + **promotional purpose** + **a
real-estate product**. It does **not** require a price, an offer, a call to action, or
contact details. Every "I'll just leave the price off" theory fails on the text.

Worse for that theory, **Art. 6(1)** obliges the marketer to *«تحديد العقار محل التسويق
عند تقديمه إلى الجمهور بدقة»* — to identify the marketed property **precisely**.
Anonymising a property to dodge licensing is itself a breach.

### But: what cannot be licensed cannot be required to be licensed

The Executive Regulations define the licence narrowly — **[OFFICIAL TEXT]**
https://rega.gov.sa/media/1mjbk4f5/اللائحة-التنفيذية-لنظام-الوساطة.pdf

> **ترخيص الإعلان العقاري:** تخويل صادر من الهيئة للإعلان ... **للتصرف في العقار** بأي وسيلة كانت.
> *An authorisation … for advertising **the disposal of the property**.*

And Art. 4(1) of the bylaw makes a **deed registration number** and **the rights being
advertised** mandatory prerequisites to applying. **[OFFICIAL TEXT]**

**[INFERENCE]** You cannot obtain a licence for a drone shot of the Corniche — there is
no deed and no rights to declare. Where REGA has provided no mechanism to license
content, that content is outside the regime. This is the defensible line, and it rests
on the text rather than on optimism.

### Two tiers, not one — and the second one is free

| | Trigger | Requirement | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1 — ad licence** | Content promoting the **disposal of an identifiable property** | Per-ad licence + Art. 5 data block (or QR) | Fee per ad (REGA publishes no figure; the widely repeated "SAR 50" is **unverified**) |
| **Tier 2 — attribution** | **Any post related to property** by a licensed broker | Name + FAL number visible | **Free** |

Tier 2 comes from the parent statute, and the prior research missed it —
**Brokerage Law Art. 10(7)** **[OFFICIAL TEXT]** https://rega.gov.sa/media/szibqk4d/نظام-الوساطة-العقارية.pdf

> يلتزم الوسيط العقاري بما يأتي: … 7- **بيان اسمه ورقم الترخيص، في أي إعلان، أو منشور متعلق بالعقار.**
> *…state his name and licence number in **any advertisement, or post, related to property**.*

It is a tariffed offence (schedule item 4,1) and it is **warning-first**. Because
compliance is free, the operational rule is simple: **the name + FAL block goes on every
single post**, licensed or not.

### Verdict per content type

| Content | Ad licence? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Corniche / skyline / city cinematography, no property | 🟢 No | No property, no deed → unlicensable |
| District guide (Al Shati, Al Rawdah, Obhur…) | 🟢 No | Same |
| Sourced market commentary | 🟢 No | Not a property ad — but see Art. 6(3) and TAQEEM below. **Always name the source and period** |
| Design / architecture inspiration, no property named | 🟢 No | Promotes no product |
| "How to buy property in KSA" education | 🟢 No | **The safest category that exists** |
| Brand introduction | 🟢 No *(REGA)* | Promotes a service — but see the brand-name note below |
| **Interior footage of a property you market — no price, no location, no CTA** | 🔴 **Yes** | **The trap.** Promotional purpose + a property. Anonymising makes it worse (Art. 6(1)) |
| …plus "DM for details" | 🔴 Yes | Contact must match the licence application exactly (Art. 7(3)) |
| …with price and district | 🔴 Yes | Full Art. 5 block or the QR substitute |
| Carousel of several properties | 🔴 Yes — **one licence per property** | Arts. 4 and 5 operate per advertisement |
| Reposting a developer's Wafi off-plan content | 🟠 Risky | See below |
| "Coming soon" teaser for an unlicensed property | 🔴 **Prohibited** | Art. 6(5) requires a valid licence to exist for the property being marketed. **Licence first, then tease** |
| Client testimonial | 🟢 No | Promotes the service. Needs PDPL consent for name/likeness |
| "SOLD" announcement | 🟢 No — **but do not publish the price** | Brokerage Law Art. 10(4) forbids disclosing transaction secrets; schedule item 2,4 = SAR 5,000 + suspension in Jeddah |

**The Wafi off-plan exemption does not transfer to a reposter.** Art. 9 exempts the
advertiser only where **the advertiser** is licensed to market **the entire** project,
and المعلن is defined as whoever *requests publication* — which is Bona the moment it
posts. **[OFFICIAL TEXT + INFERENCE]** Safest route: share the developer's own post
natively without added promotional framing, leaving the developer as المعلن.

### The practical consequence for this plan

The trap above is the single most important operational finding, and it reshapes the
content calendar: **any footage of a property Bona actually markets is Tier 1**, however
artfully cropped. The 318 stills in `public/listings/` are not free content.

The fix is cheap and it is the opposite of waiting. **Art. 5(2) expressly allows a short
form built for exactly this medium** — **[OFFICIAL TEXT]**

> يجوز أن يقتصر مضمون الإعلان العقاري على **رقم رخصة الإعلان ورمز استجابة سريع** يتضمن الوصول إلى البيانات المشار إليها في الفقرة (1).
> *The advertisement may be limited to **the licence number and a QR code** giving access to the Art. 5(1) data.*

So: **issue ad licences for the 5–7 featured Jeddah listings in week 1.** That unlocks
the whole image library for sensory and walkthrough content, with nothing on screen but
a licence number and a QR. Until those licences exist, week 1 runs on genuinely
property-free content — which is what the calendar in §3 does.

### The disclosure blocks

**Every post (Tier 2) — burn this into the template:**
```
[الاسم] — وسيط عقاري مرخص · رخصة فال 1100313556
محتوى تعريفي عام ولا يمثل إعلاناً عقارياً عن عقار محدد.
```
> [Name] — Licensed real estate broker · FAL licence 1100313556
> General informational content; not an advertisement for a specific property.

The second line is not required by any text; it is cheap self-documentation of your
position if an inspector asks. **[INFERENCE]**

**Property post, short form (Art. 5(2)) — for Reels, Stories, Snaps, TikTok:**
```
ترخيص الإعلان: [XXXXXXXX]   [QR → صفحة بيانات المادة الخامسة كاملة]
```

**Property post, full form — for captions and the website:**
```
الوسيط المرخص: [الاسم الثلاثي كما في الرخصة]
رقم رخصة فال: 1100313556
رقم ترخيص الإعلان: [XXXXXXXX]
تاريخ انتهاء ترخيص الإعلان: [YYYY/MM/DD]
للتواصل: [الرقم المسجَّل في طلب الترخيص]
```

### TAQEEM — the one item here that carries a prison term

**[OFFICIAL TEXT]** Accredited Valuers Law (M/43) —
https://taqeem.gov.sa/web/content/portal.rules/8/attachment_ar

Art. 34(أولاً)(5) refers to the **criminal court** anyone —
> اســتعمال وســيلة مــن وســائل الدعايــة يكــون مــن شــأنها حمــل الجمهــور عــلى الاعتقــاد بأحقيتــه فــي مزاولــة مهنــة التقييــم خلافــاً للحقيقــة
> *using any means of **publicity** liable to lead the public to believe he is entitled to practise valuation, contrary to the truth*

with penalties up to **SAR 200,000 and one year's imprisonment** (Art. 32).

| Statement | Verdict |
|---|---|
| «متوسط سعر المتر في حي الشاطئ … — بيانات وزارة العدل، الربع الثاني 2026» | 🟢 Allowed — an attributed public statistic. Always name source and period |
| «سعر الطلب لهذا العقار …» (the owner's asking price) | 🟢 Allowed — a fact about the listing |
| «هذه الفيلا **تساوي** …» / «القيمة السوقية للعقار …» | 🔴 Violation — a value estimate for a specific property |
| An AVM widget on a listing page | 🔴 Violation — confirms the existing standing rule |
| **«تقييم مجاني لعقارك»** as a lead magnet | 🔴🔴 **The highest-risk phrase in Saudi real-estate marketing.** Near-verbatim Art. 34(5). **Ban it from every template** |
| Using the title «مقيّم» or offering «تقييم عقاري» | 🔴 Violation — Art. 34(6) |

Safe substitutes: «تقدير سعر الطلب» or «تحليل مقارن للأسعار المعلنة في الحي», sourced.

### Four corrections to the prior research

1. **SAR 200,000 is a statutory ceiling for the whole Brokerage Law, not the advertising tariff.** The actual schedule **[OFFICIAL TEXT]** puts a Jeddah (Class A) first offence at **a warning for the disclosure breach (item 4,1)** and **SAR 5,000 for unlicensed practice (item 1,1)**, doubling on repetition within three years. A Saudi lawyer quoted in Al-Weeam, 19 April 2026, put the realistic maximum at **SAR 40,000** — https://www.alweeam.com.sa/1269453/2026/ **[OFFICIAL OTHER]**. Right-sizing this matters: nothing here justifies staying off social media.
2. **The brand-name conflict is a Ministry of Commerce issue, not an Art. 5/7 breach.** Art. 7(3) prohibits only **mismatched contact data** — it says nothing about names or brands. **[OFFICIAL TEXT]** An account called "Bona" is fine as far as REGA is concerned, provided every property post names the licensed individual with his FAL number and the contact number matches the licence exactly. Avoid entity words (شركة، مؤسسة، "our team", "LLC") until the CR exists. No REGA guidance on brand names could be found — treat this as reasoned inference, not settled practice.
3. **Art. 8's platform obligations do not reach Instagram, Snapchat or TikTok.** They bind «المنصات العقارية الإلكترونية», defined as platforms **licensed by REGA**. **[OFFICIAL TEXT]** Consequence: there is no automated takedown pipeline on social media — Aqar will structurally block an unlicensed listing, Instagram will not. Enforcement there is **manual and complaint-driven**, and in a small Jeddah luxury market the complainant is usually a competitor. The public ad-licence inquiry service means anyone can check your posts.
4. **The Wafi exemption does not transfer to a reposter** (above).

### One action the prior research missed entirely

**Verify that FAL licence 1100313556's activity scope actually covers التسويق العقاري /
الإعلانات العقارية, not الوساطة alone.** Executive Regulations Art. 2(2)
**[OFFICIAL TEXT]**: *«تقتصر ممارسة النشاط للمرخص له في حدود الترخيص»* — practice is
confined to the licence's scope, and real-estate **marketing, advertising and
consultancy/analytics** are separately enumerated real-estate services in the Brokerage
Law. If the licence covers brokerage only, running a marketing programme is schedule
item 1,4 (*مخالفة أحكام الترخيص*), which escalates to **suspension and revocation**
rather than a fine. Five-minute check on منصة فال; real consequences.

### Enforcement history (superseded in part by the update below)

REGA referred an unlicensed advertisement circulated on social media to its committee on
**19 April 2026** (https://ajel.sa/local/z36zupgghu) — but that predates the bylaw and
was enforced under the older ضوابط. **No confirmed, dated enforcement action under the
new bylaw since 1 May 2026 could be found.** That is a limit of this research, not
evidence of tolerance. Art. 12 gives the bylaw effect from publication with **no grace
period in the text**. Hotline 199011; cs@rega.gov.sa.

### Update — enforcement IS active, and there is a second regulator to check

Two findings arrived after the section above was written and they sharpen it.

**1. REGA is actively enforcing, and said so six days ago.** **[NEWS]** Saudi Gazette,
**2 September 2026**: REGA warned that advertising violations bring licence suspension or
revocation plus **fines up to SR 200,000**, and listed *"failing to display the licensee's
name or licence number in property advertisements"* among the violations it is detecting.
It reported **17,182 field audits of advertising signs and real-estate offices in August
2026 alone** —
https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/664258/saudi-arabia/rega-warning-real-estate-violators-face-license-cancellation-sr200000-fine

This **corrects the "no confirmed enforcement found" note above**: enforcement is live and
publicised. Two caveats keep it in proportion — those audits were of **physical signs and
offices**, not social accounts, and the SR 200,000 remains the statutory ceiling rather
than the scheduled first-offence tariff. But the specific violation REGA named is the
**free** one: missing name and licence number. Fixing that costs nothing (§ disclosure
blocks above) and it is exactly what they are looking for.

**2. ⚠️ Open item — the GAMR "Mawthooq" licence may apply to the account itself.**
**[REGULATOR]** The General Authority for Media Regulation licenses *"providing
advertising content through social media platforms"*, and a binding condition of that
licence is *"not displaying any advertisements except through an account registered with
the Authority and linked to the licence"* —
https://gmedia.gov.sa/en/services/licensing-for-providing-advertising-content-through-social-media-platforms-trusted

**This is a second regulator that the prior research did not cover at all**, and the
condition attaches to the **account**, not just the post. Separately, REGA's spokesperson
stated at the Third Real Estate Brokerage Forum (Riyadh, 18 July 2026) that a Mawthooq

**What is genuinely unresolved:** whether a licensed broker advertising *their own*
contracted listings needs Mawthooq at all, or whether it binds only those selling
advertising services to third parties. I could not settle this from primary sources.
**Treat it as an open question for the lawyer**, alongside the FAL scope check. It does
not block the 65% of the calendar that advertises no property.

## 7. Paid — costs, and the cheapest test that tells you anything


### First, a correction to the prior research

The figure **"luxury real estate CPL GCC $180–450"** carried in
`2026-09-06-saudi-re-acquisition-research.md` **is not present on the page it cites**
(thehovi.com). That page's actual KSA Meta row is a general **$10–35 CPL** with no
real-estate or luxury row at all. Four independent sources put **GCC real-estate form
leads at roughly $3–55**, not $180–450:

| Source | KSA/GCC real-estate CPL | Label |
|---|---|---|
| 23HubLab, premium residential KSA | $21–53 form lead; **$40–93 qualified** | [BLOG] |
| hikmahaiagency, KSA Instagram real estate | CPA SAR 90–180 ($24–48) | [BLOG] |
| Profydigital, Dubai Meta Q1 2026 | $3.37–13.40 form lead | [BLOG] |
| thehovi.com, KSA Meta general | $10–35 | [BLOG] |

**[INFERENCE]** The reconciliation is almost certainly a unit mismatch: $180–450 is
plausible for a **qualified, appointment-ready** lead, not a raw form fill. This matters
because a budget model built on $180–450 per *lead* would have been 4–10× too pessimistic
and would have argued against ever testing paid. **Budget on cost per qualified lead and
cost per booked viewing. Never on cost per form lead** — in KSA real estate those are
cheap and mostly junk.

### Cost per follower — the surprise finding

Two platforms publish real numbers and they converge:

| Platform | Cost per follower | Geography | Source | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | **SAR 0.21 ($0.056)** | **Saudi Arabia** | Oud Alyafie case study, 14.7K followers, Spark Ads + follower objective — https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/generate-results-during-ramadan-metap | **[OFFICIAL]** |

**At SAR 0.21 per follower, 1,000 TikTok followers costs about SAR 210.** Read against
the organic targets in §5 — where 1,000 followers is roughly two months of daily posting
— that is a genuinely material option.

**And here is the part that makes it usable today:** TikTok's Community Interaction
objective runs **only on Spark Ads**, i.e. it boosts *your own organic posts*. Boost a
**DISTRICT or RULES post** — content that names no property — and **[INFERENCE]** you are
not advertising a real-estate product, so no REGA advertising licence is engaged; only
the free Tier-2 attribution block applies (§6). You can buy followers legally, cheaply,
and without a single ad licence, by promoting the exact content this plan already tells
you to make.

Two cautions. Followers bought against a follower objective are lower-intent than
followers earned by a video someone watched to the end — they inflate the number and
dilute the engagement rate that drives future organic reach. And **there is no Meta
cost-per-follower equivalent**: Instagram has no follower objective, its boost goals are
profile visits / website visits / DMs, and no credible cost-per-follower figure for Meta
exists from any source.

### KSA CPM — there is no audited benchmark, and you should stop looking for one

Varos, Gupta Media, Statista, eMarketer, Triple Whale, Revealbot, Adamigo and Adligator
were all checked: **none publishes a country-level Saudi CPM.** Revealbot states outright
that its data covers US-targeted campaigns only; Triple Whale's median is global US-DTC.
At least one Arabic "Saudi benchmark" page (racting.com) admits in its own methodology
that it took global data and "calibrated" it. Published "KSA Meta CPM" ranges from $1.23
to $15.00 — a 12× spread, which is noise, not a benchmark.

The one genuinely official KSA CPM found: TikTok's Ramadan 2026 SMB playbook, Lavera
Clinics (KSA) — **CPM SAR 4.61, CPC SAR 1.32** **[OFFICIAL]**, an order of magnitude
below the agency "benchmarks", though a self-selected success story.

Directional planning ranges only, unaudited: Meta **SAR 15–34**, TikTok **SAR 11–30**,
Snapchat **SAR 7.50–22.50**, YouTube **SAR 19–45** per 1,000 impressions.

**The only way to get a real KSA CPM is first-party.** Low fixed-budget reach campaigns
on each platform, CPM read out of Ads Manager weekly, builds a proprietary index in about
eight weeks that beats anything published.

### Seasonality — and a warning about the next fortnight

- **Saudi National Day, Wed 23 September 2026 — 15 days from now.** Discount-search interest roughly **+70%** and Visa spending **+21%** for the week (Google MENA × Visa — https://campaignme.com/winning-the-saudi-national-day-consumer/ **[INDUSTRY, platform-authored]**); agency estimates put CPM **+30–60%** **[BLOG]**. **Do not start a paid test in that window.**
- **Ramadan** (2027 begins ~mid-February): converging honest range **+30% to +70% on Meta CPM**, Eid al-Fitr week the single most expensive of the year. One counter-signal worth holding: Microsoft's own country-disaggregated Ramadan 2025 data found **Saudi the mildest market in its set** (+11% vs Qatar +146%) — https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/february-2026/decoding-ramadan-2026-data-driven-strategies-for-the-middle-east-turkiye-and-africa **[OFFICIAL]**
- Calendar: National Day 23 Sep → Riyadh Season from ~Oct → White Friday late Nov → global Q4 peak → **January trough** → Ramadan from ~mid-Feb 2027.
- **No KSA CPM index by month exists**, so every "cheap window" claim — including the January one — is unverified.

### Does boosting seed organic reach? No.

This is folklore built on a real fact. The real fact is **attribution**: TikTok Spark Ads
place paid views, likes and follows onto the organic post's counters
(https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads **[OFFICIAL]**). The invented step is that
the ranker then rewards it. That appears in no platform documentation.

The decisive evidence: TikTok sells paid-organic coupling as an **opt-in, geo-restricted
feature** with a claimed ~8% view-through-rate lift
(https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-paid-and-organic-optimization-for-brand-ads
**[OFFICIAL]**). *If paid engagement automatically seeded organic distribution, there
would be nothing to switch on.*

**Budget as if reach ends the day spend ends.** Boost only content that already
outperformed organically — and note that boosted audiences are measurably more passive
(one 173,000-post Instagram dataset found boosted Reels drew ~4× the reach but about a
third of the engagement of the same posts organically — Dash Social, June 2026,
https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/when-to-social-media-boost **[INDUSTRY]**; that study has
no control group and cannot answer the causal question).

### The recommended test — SAR 15,000 over six weeks, starting after 1 October

Run it **only after day 30**, and **not** in the National Day window.

**Stage 1 — can the offer buy attention? (2 weeks, SAR 4,200)**
- Meta/Instagram Reels, **Traffic objective optimised for Landing Page Views**, Highest Volume bidding — $50/day × 14
- Snapchat, video views / traffic — $30/day × 14
- **Gate: cost per landing-page view.** Expect $0.50–1.50. **Rebuild the creative if above $2.50 (SAR 9.40).**

**Stage 2 — will attention become conversations? (4 weeks, SAR 10,500)**
- Winner platform. **Meta click-to-WhatsApp optimised for "conversations started"** — $100/day × 28
- At ~$10 per conversation that is ~70/week, which clears Meta's learning-phase threshold of *"about 50 results in the week"* (https://www.facebook.com/business/help/112167992830700 **[OFFICIAL]**), so delivery actually optimises instead of staying learning-limited
- Click-to-WhatsApp is the right event because in KSA it is simultaneously high-frequency *and* a real buying signal. Form leads are neither.

⚠️ **Compliance constraint on this design:** Art. 5(1)(د) requires the contact method on
the ad to match the licence application exactly. **The WhatsApp number used in the test
must be the number filed on the licence** — no rotating call-tracking numbers.

**The go/no-go rule — decide on cost per booked viewing, not CPL:**

> **GO if cost per booked viewing ≤ SAR 2,000, with at least 10 viewings booked.**

The justification is unit economics, not benchmarks. A SAR 4,000,000 Jeddah unit at the
2.5% capped commission is **SAR 100,000**. At a 1-in-10 viewing-to-close rate a booked
viewing is worth **SAR 10,000**; paying SAR 2,000 is a 5× return, and the headroom
swamps any CPL figure in this document. **The real question was never "is CPL $180 or
$450" — it is what fraction of leads become viewings.** That is what the test measures.

**Why ~25–30 conversions is the threshold, and 50 is not the same 50:** Meta's 50 is an
*algorithm* requirement for exiting the learning phase. Your *decision* threshold is
statistical — the confidence interval on a count runs ±88% at 5 events, ±62% at 10,
**±36% at 30**, ±28% at 50. Thirty conversions distinguishes SAR 750 per viewing from
SAR 3,000 per viewing, which is the decision you actually face. It will not distinguish
SAR 750 from SAR 950, and you do not need it to.

**The absolute cheapest smoke test:** Snapchat at its $5/day floor for 28 days —
**$140 (SAR 525)**. That buys roughly 28,000 impressions, ~235 clicks, ~5 leads.
**Statistically useless (±88%).** Use it to prove the tracking pipe works end to end,
never to make the decision.

## 10. The "do not bother" list


Ranked by how much damage it does, not by how much time it wastes. The top three are not
merely ineffective — the platforms name them as reasons they stop recommending your
account, which for an account at zero is the same as switching growth off.

1. **Buying followers or engagement. This is the single most damaging thing you could do.**
   **[PLATFORM]** Instagram's Recommendations Guidelines exclude accounts that have
   *"repeatedly engaged in misleading practices to build followings, such as purchasing
   'likes'"* — https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232 For a zero-follower account,
   Explore and Reels recommendation is the *only* growth channel that exists. Enforcement
   is not theoretical: TikTok's own Q1 2026 transparency dataset reports **6.1 billion fake
   followers and 13.3 billion fake likes removed**, and in Saudi Arabia specifically a
   **97.4% proactive removal rate** on deceptive-behaviour content, **70.1% removed before
   any views** — https://www.tiktok.com/transparency/en/community-guidelines-enforcement
2. **Engagement pods.** **[PLATFORM]** Instagram names them almost verbatim as
   not-recommendable: *"coordinated comment networks intended to artificially drive
   engagement and distribution."* Meta's Spam standard covers barter, not just purchase.
3. **Follow/unfollow churn.** **[PLATFORM]** Meta's Spam standard bans *"Posting, sharing,
   engaging with content or creating accounts… either manually or automatically, at very
   high frequencies"*, and applies restrictions *"at lower frequencies when other
   indicators of Spam… are present"* — https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/
   **Staying under a "safe daily limit" is not a defence, and Instagram publishes no such
   limit** — every number circulating comes from vendors selling the tools.
4. **Giveaways for followers — the standard mechanic is a policy violation, not just
   low-quality.** **[PLATFORM]** Meta's Pages/Groups/Events policy: *"Your promotion must
   not require or incentivize participants to share, repost, tag others, or in any other
   way publicize your promotion"* — https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/pages_groups_events
   Tag-a-friend, share-to-story and follow-to-win are all explicitly out. Meta also
   requires registering promotions with the relevant authorities, which in KSA means
   Ministry of Commerce rules on top. And commercially it is still wrong: it buys people
   who want a prize, not an SAR 8,000,000 villa.
5. **Reposting developer renders or other brokers' reels.** **[PLATFORM]** Instagram:
   *"Unoriginal content that is largely repurposed from another source with only minor,
   immaterial edits."* TikTok: *"unoriginal or reused material without anything new"* →
   For You ineligible. A faceless account that runs on scraped developer renders
   disqualifies itself from the only feed that matters.
6. **Cross-posting a watermarked TikTok export.** See §4 — documented, live, and
   *extended* on 30 April 2026 to cover photos and carousels. Re-export clean per platform.
7. **Hashtag stuffing.** Not just dead — **physically impossible on Instagram since
   December 2025**, which caps posts at **5 hashtags**. `marketing/hashtags.md` still says
   15–20 and needs rewriting. Keep the long Arabic sets for TikTok and Snapchat, where
   hashtags remain documented ranking inputs.
8. **Facebook posting.** Create the Page — you need it for Instagram API publishing and
   Meta ads — then never post to it. **[VENDOR]** Socialinsider measures Page engagement at
   **0.15%**, and KSA Facebook is **75.7% male**, the most skewed platform in the Kingdom.
9. **LinkedIn for B2C property.** 75.3% male; the 12.0M KSA figure is *registered members*,
   not monthly actives; **[VENDOR]** Metricool measured impressions **−23%** and
   interactions **−14%** as posting rose — one of the few places where posting more
   genuinely does cannibalise. And its distribution model is person-branded, which is
   doubly wrong for a faceless account. Two posts a month, B2B only.
10. **X as a growth channel.** **[PLATFORM]** Roughly half the timeline is reserved for
    accounts the viewer already follows — structurally hostile to a cold account. KSA X is
    **shrinking (−6.8% YoY)** and engagement is **0.10–0.12%**, the lowest measured
    anywhere. Ten minutes a week for brand completeness.
11. **Threads.** 1.55M KSA users — **6.2% of adults**, about a sixteenth of Snapchat's
    reach — 69.9% male, and capped at one hashtag. Cross-post if free; staff it never.
12. **Optimising the posting minute.** For a zero-follower, recommendation-driven account,
    timing is close to irrelevant. **[VENDOR]** Buffer's own conclusion: *"Timing isn't as
    critical as it was in the chronological-feed days."* Land inside 20:30–23:00 and move on.
13. **Cold DM blasting.** **[PLATFORM]** There is no compliant API route to DM people who
    have not messaged you first. *"Comment «فيلا» and I'll send the brochure"* is compliant
    and works; blasting risks the account.
14. **A blog on the site before the video engine runs.** SEO for Jeddah property keywords is
    a 6–12 month fight against portals whose domain authority you cannot match this year.
    The video library is the content strategy for now.
15. **Waiting for the CR or establishment FAL before posting anything.** Two thirds of this
    calendar can never require an advertising licence, and an account called "Bona" is fine
    under REGA today provided property posts name the licensed individual and his FAL number
    (§6). But do not make the *reverse* mistake either — posting property footage before its
    licence exists because it "doesn't look like an ad" is exactly the trap in §6.
16. **Reading SAR 200,000 as the price of a mistake.** It is the statutory ceiling for the
    entire Brokerage Law. The published schedule puts a Jeddah first offence at a **warning**
    for a missing disclosure block and **SAR 5,000** for unlicensed practice (§6). Comply
    properly; do not let a misread ceiling cost you the channel.
